Raise Clock — App Map

Follow one action: you change “Cash on hand”.

  1. index.html — the form field name="cash" fires an input event.
  2. src/app.js › update() — reads the form, runs validate(), saves to localStorage (raiseclock.v1).
  3. src/model.js › project() — walks 36 months: closing = opening − G&A − drill; records buffer breach, zero-cash month and flow-through spend.
  4. src/model.js › headline() — raise-by = breach month − lead time.
  5. src/chart.js › renderChart() and boardNote() paint the result.

Practice to reuse: keep the money math in one pure, tested module (tests/model.test.js) and let the UI only read and paint.
